# Assignment: Coordination Demo #1

**Points:** 15 · **Due:** Wednesday, Week 3, 11:59 PM · **Submission:** online text entry (links)

## Overview

First working demo testing your team's thesis. Recommended shape: an **RFI/change-order triage agent** that ingests project correspondence (RFIs, emails, meeting minutes — real if your partner permits, synthetic otherwise) and produces structured triage: which items need response, from whom, by when, at what contractual risk.

Your demo must connect to your thesis — a beautiful tool that doesn't test the thesis is a reject.

## Deliverables

Submit links to:

1. **Live demo or recorded walkthrough** of the working system.
2. **GitHub repo** updated with: code, data, LLM prompts used, dependencies. If data is synthetic, include the generation script and a justification note.
3. **Demo note** (≤500 words): what the demo tests, what happened, what it says about your thesis.
4. Individual contribution statements: `[student name], [section], [contribution]`
