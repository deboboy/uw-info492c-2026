# Assignment: RFI Lab Deliverables

**Points:** 15 · **Due:** Wednesday, Week 1, 11:59 PM · **Submission:** online text entry (links to docs/repo)

## Overview

This assignment captures the Week 1 RFI Lab. Teams are paired: one team acts as the **General Contractor (GC)**, the other as the **Subcontractor**. The GC team designs a new UW Information School building at the program-brief level and publishes a formal RFI to the subcontractor team; the subcontractor team responds with a complete response package.

## GC Team Deliverables

1. **Program brief** (2–3 pages): site description, program spaces, structural system, major divisions of work (CSI divisions), key trade interfaces.
2. **Formal RFI** including: RFI number, date, subject, reference documents/spec sections, the question, options considered, cost/schedule impact assessment, response deadline, and required response format.
3. **Addendum** (if issued) per the lab rules.
4. **Communication log** — timestamped record of every exchange.

## Subcontractor Team Deliverables

1. **RFI response package**: direct answer, assumptions made, cost/schedule implications, supporting references.
2. **Communication log** — timestamped record of every exchange.

## Individual Deliverables (all students)

- A **debrief memo** (300–500 words, individual): where did information flow break down in the lab, what did it cost (time, clarity, risk), and how could an AI agent have helped or hurt at each step? This memo is the seed of your team's thesis.
- Your contribution statement: `[student name], [section], [contribution]`

## Grading

Graded individually. Accept/reject with comments; if rejected, 1 week to resubmit. The RFI/response quality is graded as a team artifact, but your debrief memo and contribution statement are graded individually.
