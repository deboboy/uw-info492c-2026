# Assignment: Make-up Assignment (Practitioner Interview)

**Points:** varies (restores credit for one missed assignment) · **Submission:** online text entry (link)

## Make-up assignment

Interview an industry practitioner (GC project engineer/manager, subcontractor PM, design-team member, owner's rep, or inspector). The interview should focus on the practitioner's perspective on how information flows through their projects — where it breaks down, what it costs, and how AI agents might change their work. The interview should also provide feedback on your team's journey this quarter exploring construction informatics and your demos.

Transcribe and summarize the interview with an LLM. Human transcription or summarization will not be accepted.

**For credit:** submit a link to a Google Doc with the transcript and summary of the interview. A video of the interview with transcript and summary is also acceptable.
