# Assignment: Thesis + Coordination Posture Design

**Points:** 10 · **Due:** Wednesday, Week 2, 11:59 PM · **Submission:** online text entry (links)

## Overview

Building on the RFI Lab debrief, your team commits to a quarter-long thesis and the coordination posture you will test through four demos.

## Deliverables

Submit a document (link to Google Doc or repo file) containing:

1. **Team thesis** (200–300 words): a specific, testable claim about where construction information flow breaks down — e.g., *"RFI response latency is driven by ambiguity in reference documents, and agent-assisted reference resolution can cut it."* Ground it in the RFI Lab and readings.
2. **Project lens**: the real partner project or public dataset your team will work from; if synthetic data is needed, describe your generation approach and its justification.
3. **Coordination posture**: the parties involved (GC, subs, design team, owner), the information artifacts in scope (RFIs, submittals, change orders, daily reports, inspection records), and the four demo concepts you plan to iterate (one line each).
4. **Team web presence**: each team builds a simple web application/site representing the team, its thesis, and its experiments — link it here.
5. Individual contribution statements: `[student name], [section], [contribution]`

## Grading

Graded individually (thesis/posture quality is the team artifact; contribution statement is individual). Accept/reject with comments; if rejected, 1 week to resubmit.
