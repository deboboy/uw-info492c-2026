## Week 2 — Project Lens + Thesis Formation

**Before class:** review your RFI Lab debrief notes and pick 1–2 candidate project lenses.

**In class:**

- **How construction information actually flows** — lecture: preconstruction (estimates, bids, constructability review), contracting (A201 general conditions, subcontracts, flow-down clauses), execution (RFIs, submittals, change orders, daily reports, meeting minutes), closeout (as-builts, O&M data, COBie).
- **Data access in construction** — public data (permits, inspections, bidding records, OSHA logs) vs. proprietary data (partner project data, Procore/PlanGrid exports, Dodge reports); NDAs and what partners can/cannot share; when and how to justify **synthetic data generation**.
- **Industry partners this quarter** — overview of partner organizations, the projects they've made available, and site-visit scheduling.
- Team working session: each team finalizes its **project lens** (a real partner project or a public project dataset) and its **thesis** about where information flow breaks down.
